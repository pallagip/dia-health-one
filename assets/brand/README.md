# DIA HEALTH ONE — Brand and social media kit

This kit extends the visual identity at https://www.diahealth.one/ and reuses the existing D logo. It does not replace the public brand.

## Brand identity

- Display name: Dia Health One. Legal name: Dia Health One, Inc.
- Positioning: Evidence-led computational research into glucose-responsive insulin.
- Purple #7155BD, ink #201F27, pale green #C8E5A8, ivory #F8F8F5, muted gray #686671.
- Type: Manrope for display and DM Mono for research labels (SVG exports include fallbacks).
- Original site mark: /assets/dia-health-one-mark.svg. dia-mark-master.svg is an identical copy.
- Do not use approved therapy, validated switch, breakthrough drug, clinically proven, safe insulin, or drug candidate claims. Public research documents are authoritative.
- NON-CLINICAL is required when discussing research updates. Explain limitations.

## Editorial rule

A news post must be supported by a public evidence source and state the method, limitations and next unknown. No private supplier information, unpublished simulations, or speculative clinical claims. Patrik should review claims that go beyond previously public material.
