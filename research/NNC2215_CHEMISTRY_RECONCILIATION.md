# NNC2215 chemistry reconciliation

**Updated:** 26 October 2026
**Decision:** Source-derived B1 glucoside and B29 linker–roof product components match the element-labeled adjacency in both corrected, model-built coordinate candidates. The B1 component has one exact mapping per state and matches the beta-D-glucose reference graph; its B1–B22 fragment mass rounds to the Nature SI value. The neutral B29 graph has 16 mappings per state and eight bond-label transfer classes. A separate, explicitly conditional pH 7.4 tri-carboxylate audit treats each carboxylate C–O pair as resonance-equivalent, reducing those eight bond-label classes to one chemistry-equivalent class per state. The attachment-boundary audit closes the three local heavy-atom joins in each candidate and checks the adjacent native-residue context, while preserving the recorded PheB1 hydrogen discrepancy. The full source-derived neutral constitutional graph is now assembled and formula-consistent under pinned RDKit residue templates. None of these results establishes a measured protonation microstate, full-system charge, force-field parameters, or a production model.

## Evidence reviewed

The 2024 synthesis route says the B29 Lys ε-amino group is first modified with O-succinimidyl pentyn-1-oxycarbonyl at pH 10.5. A B1 O-peracetyl-D-glucoside active ester is coupled near pH 7.5 and then deacetylated. The B29 terminal alkyne is joined to the macrocycle roof propyl azide by copper-catalysed azide–alkyne cycloaddition. The resulting product is reported as C325H461N79O97S6. The article identifies the B29 Lys attachment and B1 Phe N-terminal glycoside attachment; the patent's Example 2 describes the same conjugate and reports the matching calculated mass-to-charge values. See the [2024 article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08042-3), its [Supporting Information](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41586-024-08042-3/MediaObjects/41586_2024_8042_MOESM1_ESM.pdf), and [WO2023144240A1 Example 2](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2023144240A1/en).

The coordinate inputs are the author-supplied, model-built open and closed structures by Eva Johansson and Daniele Granata, [“3D models of NNC2215”](https://springernature.figshare.com/articles/dataset/3D_models_of_NNC2215/26526460), Figshare DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.26526460, licensed CC BY 4.0. The PDBs linked in this report are modified coordinate candidates derived from those files; they are not the original author files or experimentally solved structures.

The numbered schemes identify six B29 linker carbons (source C3–C8) and the three-carbon roof propyl-azide tail (source C21–C23). Formula arithmetic in [the existing audit](data/nnc2215-model-formula-audit.json) gives a desB30 insulin parent of C253H376N64O75S6 and an attachment difference of C72H85N15O22. The B1 attachment accounts for C10O8 heavy atoms. The B29 linker–roof appendage, excluding the LysB29 NZ endpoint, consists of the reported C56H63N15O12 macrocycle roof propyl azide plus the C6H6O2 B29 linker after the NHS leaving group departs, giving C62N15O14 heavy atoms.

The publisher's model PDBs have a 113-node/120-edge heavy-atom adjacency match for the selected PHX/LYX/MC and attachment scope, after documented open/closed LYX name aliases. [The connectivity audit](data/nnc2215-connectivity-audit.json) confirms that these records give adjacency only; PDB CONECT/LINK data do not encode bond order or formal charge.

## Two-carbon discrepancy

Both source model states contain 64 carbon, 15 nitrogen and 14 oxygen records in the LYX/MC B29-linked pool, against 62 carbon, 15 nitrogen and 14 oxygen expected from the synthesis-derived formula. The two-carbon surplus is present in both open and closed models.

The [candidate linker map](data/nnc2215-linker-mapping-audit.json) follows the six-carbon B29 linker and the three-carbon roof tail through serial-level PDB connectivity. Under that candidate partition, two tail carbons fall in LYX and one falls in MC, leaving 54 source macrocycle carbons expected in MC. The PDB MC residue has 56 carbon records.

The [coordinate-reconciliation candidate](data/nnc2215-carbon-reconciliation-candidate.json) removes MC C27/C29 and their four hydrogens, then connects the retained methyl carbons C28/C30 directly to aryl carbons C73/C75. This matches the published direct aryl methyl substituents and the SI's six-proton H-18 assignment. An independent enumeration of pairwise contractions among 18 degree-two carbon vertices found that C27/C29 is the only pair that yields the source-derived graph, in both states, within that edit class. The corrected B29-linked pool becomes C62N15O14 in both states, and the full heavy-atom inventory becomes C325N79O97S6. This is a source-supported coordinate-repair inference, not an author-provided atom map.

The candidate PDBs reopen successfully with all connectivity references resolved, no duplicate atom names within residues, strictly increasing atom serials, and records no longer than 80 columns. Both new aryl-methyl bonds are 1.50 Å and their C-H bonds are 1.09 Å; the local methyl-hydrogen overlap screen passes. In the open model, ASN A21's misplaced HXT record was removed and an OXT was reconstructed in the CA-C-O plane (C-OXT 1.25 Å; O-C-OXT 119.66°). These checks validate file consistency and local geometry only. They do not validate the full graph, stereochemistry, protonation, or force-field energy.

The synthesis schemes and patent establish the intended product, linker chemistry, reaction order and product composition. The source-derived B29 linker–roof component has an exact element/adjacency match to each corrected candidate, but the scaffold mapping and neutral bond-label transfer remain ambiguous. The candidate methyl correction remains a source-supported inference, not an authoritative atom map. The original publisher PDBs still have a two-carbon residual and the open model lacks ASN A21 OXT; these are properties of those source files, not of the corrected candidates.

## Provisional chemical assignments

These are source-informed expectations for further chemistry review, not final force-field assignments:

- The B29 Lys ε-amino group is converted to a carbamate in the pentynyl linker. The source product drawing supports the 1,4-disubstituted 1,2,3-triazole; the candidate PDBs do not independently establish that bond labeling.
- The B1 Phe α-amino group is acylated by the glucoside linker and is neutral in that conjugated form. The deprotected β-D-glucoside graph and mapped C1–C5 stereochemistry are now verified against the source scheme, the SI anomeric coupling, and a beta-D-glucose reference structure. PDB adjacency does not establish stereochemistry.
- The macrocycle has three reported carboxylic-acid groups. This audit models all three as carboxylates in a declared pH 7.4 scenario; that is an assumption, not a measured dominant microstate or a pKa result. The conditional ledger below records the group-level whole-conjugate candidate; a simulation-ready charge assignment still depends on site-specific microstate review and atom-level parameterization.
- The existing isolated PaG audit supports the singly deprotonated glucuronate, net charge −1, and five CIP labels S, S, R, S, S. It validates the isolated ligand files only; it does not validate the NNC2215 conjugate.
- A simulation condition requires a reviewed protonation inventory across terminal groups, ionizable side chains, disulfides, and conjugation sites. The conditional ledger below records a conventional group-level candidate but does not establish local pKa shifts, measured populations, or atom-localized charges. A pH label alone does not define a simulation-ready inventory.

The current PDB records also disagree around hydrogen ownership: the closed model assigns a nearby PheB1 N hydrogen to PHX without a CONECT edge, and the LYX CD2 hydrogen-neighbour count differs between states. These are additional unresolved topology records.
