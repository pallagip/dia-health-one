Evidence ladder

What is actually established?

Evidence levels run from E0 concept to E6 preclinical candidate. A claim cannot inherit confidence from another molecule or another observable.

E5 · PUBLISHED FUNCTION

NNC2215 is glucose responsive.

Published receptor, cellular and animal studies establish glucose-responsive activity for NNC2215.

E5 · PUBLISHED LIABILITY

PaG makes NNC2215 non-viable.

Direct binding, receptor assays, pig-clamp data and modeling establish a clinically relevant PaG liability.

E2 · COMPUTATIONAL HYPOTHESIS

MC-Q1 identifies where to test.

The contact-versus-distal distinction survives denser geometric sampling. This does not predict affinity.

E0 · UNMEASURED

No material exists. Public methoxy-roof data are a negative prior, not confirmation.

Compute-plan correction

Do not buy false precision.

Boltz-2 is not the affinity engine for this problem. Its documented affinity mode is for a small-molecule ligand against a protein target. GluHUT binding glucose or PaG is a synthetic host–guest system.

Boltz also does not accept concentration as a physical variable. Repeating predictions with labels such as 3, 5, 10 and 20 mM cannot create a Hill curve.

The corrected AWS plan first reproduces the public GluHUT explicit-solvent umbrella-sampling benchmark, then calibrates parent glucose-versus-PaG behavior, and only then compares MC-Q1 and controls prospectively.

Confidence-changing work

The next gates.

Every gate has a stop rule. More compute is not progress when the method or chemical object has not passed the preceding gate.

C1 · Reproduce public GluHUT analysis

Reanalyse deposited trajectories and regenerate published PMFs and uncertainty before generating new trajectories.

C2 · Validate chemistry and force field

Resolve exact parent graph, charges, nonstandard terms, protonation and PaG treatment.

C3 · Calibrate parent glucose versus PaG

Use multiple explicit-solvent paths. Require stable direction, overlap and uncertainty smaller than the effect.

C4 · Prospective candidate comparison

Without retuning, compare MC-P0, MC-Q1, MC-Q1D, MC-P3 and preferably MC-P1.

C5 · Provider acceptance

Obtain a written route, positional assignment, material specification, price and rights/data terms.

C6 · Direct binding

Use two orthogonal methods. This is the first gate capable of turning MC-Q1 into a measured hit.
